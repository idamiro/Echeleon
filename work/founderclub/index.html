01Client project

FounderClub — a clearer home for a business community.

Product structure, UI/UX and development for a platform connecting membership, events, news and community activity.

The work focused on giving events, news and membership a consistent hierarchy — without losing the local identity that makes FounderClub feel like Baku, not a generic SaaS template.

Client
FounderClub
Role
Product structure, UI/UX, development
Duration
Approx. six weeks
Status
Live at founderclub.az

01 / Context

Different activities needed to feel like one platform.

FounderClub brings together a business community, events, editorial news and membership. The website had to make those parts easy to understand while keeping a premium, locally grounded identity.

02 / Response

A structured editorial system — not disconnected pages.

The interface uses a consistent hierarchy for announcements, event material and community content. Strong imagery carries identity; restrained typography and predictable navigation carry the information.

Events and editorial content share the same visual language — photography leads, copy supports.

03 / Outcome

A coherent platform that is live and ready to use.

Membership, events and editorial content now share one navigable system. The delivered website gives FounderClub a consistent public face — live at founderclub.az.
