# Design direction — Vulcet × Founder Club LinkedIn carousel

## System

| Token | Value | Role |
|-------|-------|------|
| Paper | `#f2eee6` | Light slides, Vulcet site ground |
| Paper light | `#faf8f3` | Media frames |
| Signal / Coal | `#07080b` / `#171715` | Dark slides, primary type |
| Graphite | `#595650` | Secondary type on paper |
| Rule | `#c9c2b7` / `#4a4843` | Editorial dividers |
| Display | Instrument Serif italic | Emphasis only (`built.`, `First step.`) |
| Sans | Inter 450–700 | Structure, labels, facts |
| Format | 1080×1350 (4:5), @2x export | LinkedIn carousel native |

## Art direction

- Editorial agency case-study language — not a Canva template grid.
- Alternating coal / paper rhythm: 01 dark → 02 photo dark → 03 paper → 04 split → 05 dark.
- Large type, hairline rules, numbered labels (`01`–`05`) matching vulcet.com editorial labels.
- No gradients-as-decoration, neon, 3D icons, pill clusters, or fake stat strips.
- Founder Club mark used unmodified (circular crop of official PNG on dark fields only).
- Vulcet wordmark inverted on dark slides; native black on paper slides.
- Photography is documentary/event: networking breakfast, office visit, live site hero.

## Slide-by-slide

1. **Cover** — Signal black field. Wordmark top-left. Oversized “The first project.” Partner lockup with FC mark.
2. **Client** — Full-bleed networking photo + heavy bottom vignette. FC identity + one-line community definition.
3. **Work** — Paper field. Fact grid from case study only + live site hero frame with “Live · founderclub.az”.
4. **Why** — Split editorial: copy + real office photo. Three numbered reasons, dual-brand footer on image.
5. **Close** — Dual marks, “First project. First step.”, two destination lines, email in footer.

## Brand balance

- Vulcet owns the system (type, paper/coal, wordmark, labels).
- Founder Club owns photography + official mark + gold/blue identity inside its logo only.
- Neither brand is recolored or restyled.
