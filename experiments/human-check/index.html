Could proving you’re human feel human?

Traditional CAPTCHA experiences often ask people to behave mechanically — scanning grids, selecting tiles, repeating until the machine is satisfied. This experiment asks whether a short, natural interaction can feel more like a conversation with the interface than a test.

It is a design experiment, not a production security replacement. No behavioral data leaves the browser.
