02Visual identity

A visual system people can recognise and the team can use.

For brands that need more than a logo: a coherent language across websites, presentations, social content and every important point of contact.

Typical timelineFrom 4 weeks

The final scope depends on the existing strategy, required applications and the level of guidance the team needs after delivery.

Good fit for

  • New brands ready for a visual direction
  • Businesses with an inconsistent identity
  • Rebrands following a strategic shift
  • Teams that need a repeatable visual system

Typical scope

  • Creative direction
  • Logo refinement or identity mark
  • Colour and typography system
  • Key visual and image direction
  • Identity guidelines
  • Motion principles
01

Set the direction

Translate the brand strategy into a focused visual territory with clear references and principles.

02

Build the key visual

Create the core relationship between mark, type, colour, imagery and composition.

03

Make it repeatable

Document the rules and applications so the identity remains coherent across teams and channels.

04

Define motion

Set purposeful animation and transition principles that extend the identity without distracting from the message.
