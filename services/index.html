03Before the proposal

Start with the need,
not the label.

Yes. The first step is to turn the idea into a clear brief: who it is for, what it needs to communicate and what the business needs it to accomplish.

Yes. The disciplines can work independently, but the strongest continuity comes from connecting the direction, visual system, interface and final build.

Start with the unresolved decision. If the position is unclear, begin with strategy. If the direction is clear but the expression is not, begin with identity or product design. Development begins once the experience and technical scope are ready.

Vulcet reviews the goals, current materials, required deliverables, functionality and timing, then provides a written scope with the recommended starting point.
