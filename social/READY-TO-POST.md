# Social post drafts — Focused website vs content platform

Editorial concept (not Vulcet service package names):

## Post angle

Two website scopes founders confuse:

**Focused website**
- Clear offer
- Credible proof
- One primary action
- Fast to launch and maintain

**Content platform**
- Deeper architecture
- Publishing rhythm
- Markets / languages
- Room for case studies and news

## Key line

Choose scope from maintenance reality — not prestige.

## Do not use

- Business website vs growth website as Vulcet service names
- Old package prices ($2,800 / $5,200)
- “Independent web design studio” positioning
