## POST 1 — Business vs growth (en güçlü hook)

### Instagram
```
Business site or growth site?

Most companies ask for a “modern website.”
But those are two different jobs:

→ clear offer + trust + contact
→ or expanding content + languages + CMS

Wrong scope = wasted budget + a site nobody maintains.

Which one are you?
Comment “business” or “growth.”

Link in bio.
```

### LinkedIn
```
Most companies don’t need a bigger website.
They need a clearer one.

A business website solves:
• offer clarity
• credibility
• a clean path to contact

A growth website solves:
• deeper architecture
• content that keeps expanding
• multilingual / CMS reality

If your team won’t publish weekly, don’t buy a publishing machine.
If sales already depend on case studies and markets, don’t ship a thin brochure.

I wrote a short guide on how we choose at Vulcet:
https://vulcet.com/blog/business-website-vs-growth-website/

Curious — which side are you on right now?
```

## POST 2 — When to redesign

**Görsel (yazılı):** `02-ig-when-to-redesign.jpg`  
**Görsel (foto):** `02b-photo-when-to-redesign.jpg`

### Instagram
```
Looking “old” isn’t always a redesign brief.

Redesign when:
• the offer still can’t be explained
• key pages are missing or buried
• mobile is broken
• sales won’t send people to the site

Refresh when:
• the structure is right, the expression is tired

Details in bio.
Save this for your next brief.
```

### LinkedIn
```
A redesign is not a new color palette.

Redesign when:
• the offer is hard to explain from the homepage
• key pages are missing
• technical debt blocks every enquiry
• competitors are clearer — not flashier

Refresh when:
• structure is right, expression is tired

I mapped the decision here:
https://vulcet.com/blog/when-a-business-website-needs-a-redesign/

If your sales team still says “ignore the website, I’ll explain” — that’s the signal.
```

### X (Twitter)
```
Redesign when the site can’t explain the business.
Refresh when it can — but looks tired.

Signal:
sales avoids sending people to the site.

https://vulcet.com/blog/when-a-business-website-needs-a-redesign/
```
